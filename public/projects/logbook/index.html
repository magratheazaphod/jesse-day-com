Logbook

A lightweight task tracker that also logs and summarizes my Claude sessions every day.

As I hit my stride with my plethora of Claude projects, I started generating a ton of additional ideas, both big and little. I decided to build my own hyper-personalized lightweight task tracker instead of using an off-the-shelf solution. Claude named it the Logbook and generated a cute icon (I was also inspired by the Lil'Log blog). Cards fall into Tasks (small) and Ideas (big). They can only be dragged into the current day, and any unfinished work falls back to the top of the pile. The Logbook summarizes all of your Claude sessions (both Code and Cowork) and then produces a brief daily summary. There's no obligation for the cards and sessions to match, because I want it to be fun, not something where I waste my limited time linking issues and feeling guilty when I forget.

Some fun learnings: Initially, the daily summaries were surprisingly critical (I don't need Claude to shame me for using fewer tokens than the day before!). Also, Claude is bad at assessing what work is more or less important, and frequently centered its daily summaries on one-liner throwaway sessions. I calibrated the summaries to skew positive and focus on completion of bigger projects and clear wins.

Current limitations: Local machine-only, Claude-only
