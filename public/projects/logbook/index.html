Logbook

A lightweight task tracker that also logs and summarizes my Claude sessions every day.

Source and install instructions on GitHub

As I hit my stride with my plethora of Claude projects, I started generating a ton of additional ideas for future work, both big and small. I find that off-the-shelf solutions like Jira, Linear, Notion and Coda come with a litany of unwanted features, which become extra baggage that dampen my creativity and enthusiasm. I also wanted more than just a Trello board because I spend 95% of my productive time in either Claude Code or Cowork, and it became a major need to track and summarize past agent sessions. The logbook has cards and auto-generated session and daily summaries. There are no story points, no epics and no obligation for cards and sessions to match.

Some fun learnings: Initially, the daily summaries were surprisingly critical (I don't need Claude to shame me for using fewer tokens than the day before!). Also, Claude is bad at assessing what work is more or less important, and frequently centered its daily summaries on one-liner throwaway sessions. I calibrated the summaries to skew positive and focus on completion of bigger projects and clear wins.

Current limitations: Local machine-only, Claude-only
